02 · Catalog

43 benchmarks, three depths of page

Most of the catalog is other people's benchmarks run by Mercor. Only 8 pages are Mercor's own or a partner's, and those are the only ones with the full treatment. Verified

03 · The extended trick

Same models, fresh tasks, half the score

Ten "Mercor-extended" pages put each model's score on a public benchmark next to its score on Mercor's held-out tasks. The drop sells fresh data without saying so. Verified

04 · Methodology spec

Eleven fields, filled on every card

The methodology page has 31 benchmark cards. Every card fills the same 11 fields. One rule applies everywhere: maximum reasoning effort, with repeats averaged into pass@1, never best-of. Verified

05 · Commercial model

The leaderboard is the shop window

The public set is licensed for evaluation only and gated on Hugging Face. Training rights to the full dataset are what Mercor sells. No price appears anywhere. Verified

Supply side

Mercor pays companies for their workplace data

mercor.com/data asks firms to connect Slack, Gmail, Salesforce, GitHub, Drive, Notion and 100+ other tools. The data is de-identified automatically and licensed to labs. Payment comes 2 to 4 weeks after signing, with referral bonuses. Target industries: legal, healthcare, finance, professional services, manufacturing, retail, real estate, logistics.

Versioning. A new version gets a new Hugging Face repo, and the blog post doubles as the changelog. The leaderboard URL is reused and old scores are replaced. APEX-Agents v1.1 removed scores for models caught "scattergunning". There are no changelog files, no pointers from old versions to new, and no DOIs.

09 · Weaknesses

Where the polish cracks

Eight visible problems. Most come from keeping the same fact in several places.

Release dates disagree

APEX-Accounting is "Released March 2026" on the methodology page. Its paper and blog are from July 2026.

Task counts disagree

APEX-Accounting: 100 tasks on its page, a 160-task private set in its paper. Verified

Expert counts disagree

"5M+ domain experts" on the data page, "4M+ experts" on the partner page. Verified

Origin is implied

APEX-Accounting sits under partners, but the methodology page credits Mercor alone. There is no origin field.

Thin third-party pages

35 of 43 pages have no uncertainty, no FAQ and no date.

No version hygiene

Old repos stay up with no pointer to the new version. No changelog files.

No quality data in the shop

The licensing catalog shows no per-dataset quality numbers. The rigor stays in the papers.

Judges from scored labs

Judges are mostly frontier models from labs that are also on the boards. Mercor discloses the risk.
