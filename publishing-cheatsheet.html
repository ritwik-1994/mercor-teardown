Cheatsheet: how Mercor publishes

Keep this open beside the walkthrough. One row per beat, the numbers to have ready, the questions leaders are likely to ask, and every term on the page.

The story in one lineMercor runs every benchmark through the same four levels: a catalog slot by ownership, a fixed page template, 12 pre-publish gates with numbers, and a fixed launch recipe. It's fast, and it slips on versioning and consistency.

Likely questions, short answers

Does Mercor publish a checklist?

No. We reconstructed it from 6 arXiv papers, which describe the same gates each time, plus repo and blog dates.

How long from first artifact to launch?

About a week at the median. Repos are created privately 0–6 days before the arXiv paper.

Do they always release open data?

On 4 of 5 launches. Twice it came 45 and 71 days after launch.

How do they handle new versions?

A new Hugging Face repo, with the blog post as the changelog. The board URL stays the same and scores are replaced. Old versions don't point to new ones.

How do partner launches work?

Two modes. Partner-first: the partner launches and Mercor hosts a board later (Harvey, Cognition, Sierra). Co-launch: same-day posts from both (PLE with Google).

What can't we see?

Who signs off a release, reviewers per task, discard rates and the difficulty threshold. Former staff and contractors could tell us.

What ships on every launch?

The arXiv paper, a blog with named authors, the leaderboard and a hidden test set: 5 of 5.

What do they never ship?

A changelog file, a DOI or release tags on launch repos.

Terms on the page

pass@1Share of tasks a model solves in one attempt, averaged over k runs.
kRuns per task. Mercor uses 3 to 8. More runs, tighter error bars.
± / CIUncertainty on a score. A gap smaller than it isn't a real lead.
Held-outTasks kept private so models can't have trained on them.
Extended benchmarkA public benchmark plus new private tasks. Shown with two scores.
HeadroomHow far the best model is from 100%. A top score of 20–30% means hard but not hopeless.
LLM judgeAn AI model that grades answers against a rubric.
Judge validationHow often the AI judge agrees with expert graders. Mercor: 97–98.5%.
κ (kappa)Agreement between graders after removing chance. 0.857 is strong.
Independent re-solveExperts who never saw a task solve it fresh, to prove it's fair and solvable.
ContaminationTest tasks leaking into training data, which inflates scores.
HarnessThe code that runs a model on a task: tools, limits, environment.
HarborA task container format. Mercor ships in it.
ArchipelagoMercor's open-source harness and environment.
Gated HF setA Hugging Face dataset you must accept terms to download.
CC-BY-4.0, eval onlyFree to use with credit, but only for evaluation. No training.
APEXMercor's benchmark brand: APEX-Agents, APEX-SWE, APEX-Accounting and others.
PLEPhysical-Engineering's Last Exam, co-launched with Google Cloud AI Research.
Spec cardMercor's 11-field methodology card for each benchmark.
Run ruleEvery model at max reasoning effort, averaged into pass@1, never best-of.
T0 / T−7Launch day, and days before it. T+14 is two weeks after.
Dev splitA small open sample of a benchmark; the rest stays held-out.
Release cardThe Hugging Face page for a dataset: license, disclaimer, citation.
EmbargoPress gets the story early but can't publish before launch.
ScattergunningSubmitting many attempts to game a board. Mercor removed scores for it.
ChangelogA record of what changed between versions. Mercor has none.

Before you present

  1. Recheck Mercor's newest models on mercor.com/apex if you present after this week.
  2. Press T to hide the talk track before you share your screen. J and K move between beats.
  3. Say the evidence label when quoting an R or I item.
VVerifiedRead on the primary source by us.
RResearchedFrom a research agent with the source cited, not re-read.
IInferredOur reading of indirect evidence. Say "it looks like".
